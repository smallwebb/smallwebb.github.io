Core Principles

/// 01

No Trackers

We don't care who you are, where you've been, or what you're doing. Analytics and tracking scripts are strictly banned from the smallwebb index.

Zero Ads

Information shouldn't be held hostage by attention merchants. No banners, no pop-ups, no sponsored placements. Just the tools and content you need.

Truly Private

Tools on smallwebb process data locally on your machine whenever possible. If data must be sent, it is end-to-end encrypted by default.

Decentralized

No single point of failure, no corporate overlords. Hosted by the community, for the community, utilizing peer-to-peer protocols.

Tired of the modern web?

Help us build the alternative. Host a node, submit a privacy-first tool, or just start browsing the directory.
