internet, as it was meant to be.

A decentralized search engine for tools and sites. Free of trackers, ads, and algorithms. Find what you need, and close the tab.

features

Local First

Tools listed here are built to run primarily in your browser or on your local machine. Your data never leaves your device unless absolutely necessary.

No Tracking

Analytics, tracking pixels, and invasive cookies are strictly prohibited. You are an anonymous visitor, not a data point to be sold.

Zero Ads

No banners, video ads or pop-ups. The directory relies on sponsors and donations to provide straightforward utilities.

Accesible

smallwebb is distributed through GitHub. Anyone can suggest changes and submit sites. Sites are local-first and lightweight.
